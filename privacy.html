Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Flip to Talk respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

Flip to Talk does not require account registration. The app does not intentionally collect personal information through its conversation card features.

2. Local Data Storage

Favorites, preferences, and session progress are stored locally on your device. This information is used to provide the app's features.

3. Conversations

Flip to Talk does not record, store, or transmit the conversations or stories shared while using the app.

4. Third-Party Services

Flip to Talk does not currently integrate advertising, analytics, or third-party crash reporting services.

5. Internet Connection

Conversation cards and completion letters are bundled with the app and can be accessed offline.

6. Children's Privacy

Flip to Talk does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.
